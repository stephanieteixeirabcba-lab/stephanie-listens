Ethics in the Room, Not on Paper

1.0 ethics

on demand · online asynchronous · published [DATE]

What to do when the plan and your clinical judgment disagree. Raising a concern within scope, documenting it, and handling the conversation when nothing changes.

Learning objectives, instructor, and disclosures

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

  1. Identify when a disagreement with a treatment plan rises to an ethical concern under the Ethics Code for Behavior Analysts.
  2. Describe two within-scope routes for raising that concern.
  3. Document a raised concern in a way that protects both the client and the practitioner.

When a Session Stops Working

1.0 general

on demand · online asynchronous · published [DATE]

A decision framework for the moment things escalate: continue, adapt, pause, or contact. Separating what you observed from what you assumed, and what to bring back to your supervisor.

Learning objectives, instructor, and disclosures

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

  1. Apply a four-option decision framework — continue, adapt, pause, or contact — to an escalating session.
  2. Distinguish observed behavior from inferred intent when describing what occurred.
  3. Summarize an escalation for a supervisor in terms that support a programming decision.

questions

Before you join.

How much will the courses cost?+

Single courses will be in the $25–45 range, with a bundle for anyone taking several at once. Early access members get founding pricing below that, held for as long as they stay on the list.

Will these count toward my recertification?+

Yes — for the BACB, IBAO, and QABA. Courses award BACB CEUs through my ACE provider authorization, including ethics and supervision hours.

Are they live or on demand?+

On demand. Watch on your schedule, pause and come back, and download the companion resource. Each course includes knowledge-check questions you answer as you go — that's how participation is verified — and your certificate issues once you've completed them.

Are they available in Portuguese?+

Portuguese versions are planned. Tell me on the form if that's what you need and it moves up the queue.

Can my agency buy these for a whole team?+

Yes. Group rates are available for clinics and agencies — choose "Agency or clinic" on the form and I'll follow up directly.
